# Smart KYC

## Documents needed
Asked as: which papers, what should I keep ready, list of documents, docs for KYC, how old can the address proof be, certificates required.
A business needs four things for KYC: the GST certificate, the Udyam (MSME) certificate, an address proof dated within the last 90 days (for example an electricity, water or phone bill in the business's name) and an ID of the authorised signatory (PAN or Aadhaar). Partnership firms, LLPs and companies must also upload an authorisation letter or board resolution naming the signatory. A business without a GSTIN can still complete KYC for products that do not need a GSTIN.

## Fastest way: DigiLocker and Aadhaar OTP
Asked as: KYC without uploading photos, quickest way, no paperwork, skip the photos, instant verification.
Instead of photos, the owner can fetch the GST and Udyam certificates from DigiLocker, which pulls them from the government source, and verify the signatory with Aadhaar OTP e-KYC. This takes about a minute and needs no paper.

## Uploading photos
Asked as: how to take the photo, the picture is blurry, photo rejected, scan quality, glare, retake.
Photos are read automatically. Take each photo in good light, flat, with all four corners visible and no glare. If a photo is too blurred to read, the app says so straight away and asks for a new photo.

## Checks we run
Asked as: what do you verify, how do you check my GST number, why was a name mismatch flagged, is my certificate genuine, fraud checks.
Each document is checked against the GST and Udyam registries and against the other documents: the GSTIN format and check digit, whether the GSTIN is active (not cancelled or suspended), that the legal name matches the registry, that the state code matches the address, that the Udyam number is valid, that the address proof is recent and matches, and that the signatory ID is valid. Every problem is explained in one plain sentence with what to do next.

## If a check fails
Asked as: my document was rejected, KYC failed, what now, cancelled GSTIN, fix and retry, verification failed, wrong document.
The owner sees exactly which document failed and why, fixes only that document and verifies again. A cancelled or suspended GSTIN cannot be used for GST-only products, but the owner can still buy internet, mobile and other products that do not need a GSTIN.
