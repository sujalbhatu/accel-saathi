# Quote, pricing and ordering

## How prices work
Asked as: how much will I pay, is tax or GST included, is the price before tax, hidden charges, one-time fee, setup charge, single bill, total cost, what the bill looks like.
Every bundle has one monthly price for all services on one bill, shown before 18% GST, plus a one-time setup fee for some products. The setup fee also carries 18% GST, so the first bill is the monthly price plus the setup fee, plus GST on both; every later bill is the monthly price plus GST. GST-registered businesses can usually claim input tax credit on the GST. Prices come from the product catalogue and are always within its price range.

## 12 or 24 months
Asked as: lock-in, contract length, commit for one year or two years, longer term cheaper, lowest price, minimum period, tenure.
The owner chooses a 12- or 24-month contract. A 24-month contract gets the lowest catalogue price for each product; the services are the same on both, only the commitment changes. Some products have their own minimum contract length, shown in the catalogue (for example the managed firewall and SD-WAN are always 24 months).

## Changing the bundle
Asked as: can I remove or drop a service, add something later, change the quantity, edit the quote, take out an add-on, modify before ordering.
Add-ons can be switched on or off, quantities changed, and the contract length switched at any time before ordering, on the quote screen or by asking the advisor (for example "make it cheaper", "add CCTV", "remove WhatsApp", "my budget is 2000"). Every change is re-checked against the catalogue rules and re-priced instantly.

## Steps to order
Asked as: how do I buy, what are the steps, the process, what happens next, sign the agreement, e-sign, place the order.
Advisor, then quote, then consent and Smart KYC, then the agreement is e-signed with Aadhaar OTP, then the order is placed. No office visit and no paper forms are needed.
