# Who can buy what

## City tier
Asked as: is it available in my city or town, why can't I get 1 Gbps here, not offered in my area, small town, metro only.
Some products are available only in tier-1 cities (for example Biz Fibre 1G). The advisor checks the city automatically and suggests the best available alternative.

## Team size and locations
Asked as: we are a small team, only a few people, minimum staff, can a small shop take a leased line, how many branches are needed, too small for this product.
Some products need a minimum team size or number of locations: Internet Leased Line 20, managed SD-WAN, managed firewall and machine monitoring need 10 or more staff; Internet Leased Line 100 needs 25 or more; Biz Fibre 1G needs 5 or more; cloud telephony needs at least 3 staff; managed SD-WAN also needs 3 or more sites.

## GSTIN
Asked as: which products need GST, what can I get without GST, no GST number, unregistered business, is GST compulsory.
These products need an active GSTIN: WhatsApp Business API, bulk SMS, toll-free numbers, cloud telephony, managed SD-WAN, managed firewall, machine monitoring, both leased lines and the 1 Gbps fibre plan. Without a GSTIN the owner can still buy internet, mobile, CCTV, POS billing, email, security, backup and more, and add the GST-only products later.

## Products that need an internet line
Asked as: do I need your internet for this, only CCTV or Wi-Fi without a line, will it work with my existing connection.
Some products work only with an internet line in the same bundle (for example static IP, backup failover, Wi-Fi with guest network, cloud CCTV). The advisor adds or keeps the right line automatically.
