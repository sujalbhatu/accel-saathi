# Installation and order tracking

## After ordering
Asked as: what happens after I order or pay, the stages of an order, will I get updates, track progress, where is my order, order status.
The order moves through: KYC verified, agreement e-signed, site feasibility confirmed, installation scheduled, installation in progress, and all services live. The owner gets a WhatsApp update at every step.

## How long it takes
Asked as: how many days, when will it start, how soon, go-live date, installation time, waiting time, delay.
Each product has a typical installation time in the catalogue (for example fibre about 5 days, 5G AirFiber about 2 days, mobile lines 1 day, a leased line 21 to 30 days). The whole bundle is live when its slowest product is installed; the advisor shows this "live in" estimate with the quote.

## Booking the technician
Asked as: when will the engineer come, choose the visit time, pick a date, appointment, schedule the installation, slot.
The owner picks the technician's slot themselves in the order tracker, no phone call needed.

## Help and tickets
Asked as: something is not working, who do I complain to, raise a complaint, support, a problem after installation, call me back, speak to a person, fault, broken.
The tracker shows every service's status and expected date. The owner can raise a ticket from the tracker, and a local Accel partner who speaks their language can call back on request.

## No fibre at the site
Asked as: no cable in my area, fibre not available, no wired connection reaches my lane, wireless option, without digging.
If fibre is not available at the address, the advisor suggests 5G AirFiber, which needs no cable and is plug-and-play.
