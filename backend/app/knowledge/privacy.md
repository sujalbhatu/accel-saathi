# Privacy and data protection (DPDP Act, 2023)

## Consent first
Asked as: do you read my documents before I agree, permission, can I say no, withdraw consent, what am I agreeing to.
No document is read before the owner gives consent on a clear screen that lists what is collected and why. Consent is recorded with its version and time. Order updates on WhatsApp and marketing offers are separate choices; marketing is off by default.

## Purpose and minimisation
Asked as: do you keep or store my photos, what do you do with my documents, how long is data kept, is my Aadhaar saved, who uses my data.
Documents are used only to verify the business and the signatory for the telecom connection. Photos are deleted right after the details are read; only a fingerprint (hash) of each file is kept to prove what was checked. ID numbers such as GSTIN and Aadhaar are masked on screen, in the partner console and in the audit log.

## Owner's rights
Asked as: can I delete or erase my data, remove my details, see what you hold about me, right to be forgotten.
The owner can see what was collected and can erase their data before placing an order. Every access to KYC data is written to an audit log.

## AI and data
Asked as: does the AI or chatbot or bot see my documents or Aadhaar photo, is the model trained on my data, does the AI decide my KYC.
The AI advisor never sees document images. It only gets the business details the owner typed, and it never decides products or prices; a rules engine does.
